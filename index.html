About the spa

A Brazilian spa in the heart of Deerfield Beach

Located on West Hillsboro Boulevard, Missio's Spa brings together advanced facial aesthetics, micropigmentation and brow design in one welcoming space.

The philosophy is simple: because no two skins are alike, every protocol is individual — designed after listening to you and understanding your skin first.

Service is offered in English and Portuguese, and appointments are booked personally over WhatsApp — the Brazilian way: warm, direct and uncomplicated.
